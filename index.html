Consideration is the quietest form of care.

A lab for open-ended projects. At the moment, that mostly means one question: what is the invisible machinery between a model and the world — the part that decides what it sees next, what its output does, and when to stop? I am working toward the smallest theory that covers all such systems, precisely enough to code.

Named for Idaea pseliota, the doily wave moth.
